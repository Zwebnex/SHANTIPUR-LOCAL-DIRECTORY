ABOUT SHANTIPUR

A City of Sacred Traditions and Living Heritage

Situated on the banks of the sacred Bhagirathi River in Nadia, West Bengal (PIN: 741404), Shantipur has flourished for over five centuries as an extraordinary convergence of Tantric scholarship, Sri Advaita Acharya's Gaudiya Vaishnava lineage, and the celebrated GI-tagged Shantipuri handloom weaving tradition.

500 Years of Sacred Shrines

Consecrated by Pandit Krishnananda Agambagish and venerable scholar families, maintaining unique living rituals including Vedic marriage rites and synchronized immersion processions.

Syncretic Architectural Skyline

Home to Bengal's monumental 1726 CE Atchala terracotta temples, the historic ancestral seat of Boro Goswami Bari, and the 1703 CE Topkhana Mosque.

GI-Tagged Handloom & Pre-Dawn Haats

A thriving 1409 CE craft lineage of over 10,000 master weavers producing 100x100 fine cotton counts, traded at the legendary Sutragarh wholesale markets.
