## 1. Executive Summary & Archival Directive

In accordance with the Shantipur Heritage Platform Master Specification, a comprehensive photographic, geographic, and theological verification of all sacred shrines, temples, deity idols, handloom clusters, and cultural guilds across Shantipur was performed.

### Strict Non-Negotiable Archival Standards:
1. **Honest Attribution:** A photograph is published only when its identity is verified through primary archival documentation, custodian temple trusts, or verified Wikimedia Commons documentation.
2. **Strict Deduplication:** Zero duplicate image assignments across independent shrines or temples.
3. **Golden Om (ॐ) Placeholder Engine:** Where authentic photographs are pending custodian trust documentation or identity cannot be corroborated, the directory automatically renders a warm cream and antique gold Om (ॐ) vector placeholder (`/images/santipur/om-placeholder.svg`), strictly eliminating blank containers and misleading substitute images.
4. **Performance & Access:** All imagery is loaded asynchronously (`decoding="async"`) with native lazy loading (`loading="lazy"`).
